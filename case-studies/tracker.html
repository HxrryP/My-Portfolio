THE PROJECT

What this project
set out to do.

I built a monthly view to see scheduled bills together, understand the total due in each month, and notice when an installment ends.

  • Monthly overview

    A calendar-like schedule puts each expected payment beside its month.

  • Running totals

    Monthly totals make the overall commitment easier to see.

  • A clearer future view

    Seeing when an installment ends helps with planning later months.
