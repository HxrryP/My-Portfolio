THE PROJECT 01 / 03

What this project
set out to do.

The published project description presents a responsive website for the Municipal Office of Anilao, Iloilo, with service information, online payment, and a recommender API among its features.

  • Finding the right service

    A service website should help a visitor identify the appropriate next step.

  • Online journey

    The described scope combines service information with online payment functionality.

  • Supporting technology

    Laravel, Bootstrap, Python, and PayMongo are the tools listed for this project.

THE SAMPLE 02 / 03

See the idea
in action.

The interactive sample lets visitors choose a service and check the information they would prepare. It illustrates a resident-facing guide; it does not send requests or process payments.
